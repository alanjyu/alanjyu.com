Original vectors drawn for Clef, independent of music fonts or third-party glyph outlines.

Each SVG exposes its root as `#glyph` for external `<use>` references. Notation inherits `currentColor`. Clefs use 24 units per staff space: the treble loop marks G at local y=108, and bass dots sit at y=16 and y=40, around F at y=28. Noteheads and accidentals are centered on the note position; stems and ledger lines stretch to their requested dimensions.
